## How to Interpret These Metrics

- **Accepted** means the question author selected the answer as the accepted answer on re:Post.
- **Expert Approved** records the corresponding review status shown by the re:Post platform.
- These are community-platform outcomes. They do not represent an official AWS certification, AWS Support confirmation, or a guarantee that a solution applies to every environment.
- The figures are a dated snapshot and may change as further questions and answers are reviewed.

## Relationship to This Repository

The purpose of this repository is not to reproduce re:Post discussions or to collect metrics alone. Selected discussions are turned into independent technical research notes that:

1. Summarize the scenario in original wording.
2. Identify the AWS services and technical concepts involved.
3. Compare claims with official AWS documentation and reliable evidence.
4. Distinguish confirmed findings from assumptions and untested hypotheses.
5. Record what was learned and link to the original public discussion.

Only discussions with a reusable technical learning point and sufficient supporting evidence should become repository entries.
